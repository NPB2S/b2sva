# BridgeToSuccess Virginia (B2SVA)

Official Web Platform for **BridgeToSuccess Virginia** ([b2sva.org](https://b2sva.org)).

BridgeToSuccess Virginia is a statewide non-profit platform connecting high school immigrant students and English Language Learners (ELLs) with volunteer peer tutors and adult mentors across the entire Commonwealth of Virginia.

## 🌟 Key Platform Features

1. **Statewide Scope Across Virginia**:
   - Serving immigrant students and English learners throughout all Virginia school divisions and communities (Northern Virginia, Central Virginia / Richmond, Hampton Roads / Tidewater, Shenandoah Valley, Roanoke / Southwest VA, and Southside Virginia).

2. **"What language do you speak?" Multi-Language Support**:
   - Welcome onboarding modal & navigation bar switcher supporting 8 languages: English, Spanish (*Español*), Arabic (*العربية*), Amharic (*አማርኛ*), Dari/Pashto (*دری / پښتو*), Mongolian (*Монгол хэл*), Vietnamese (*Tiếng Việt*), and Urdu (*اردو*).

3. **Fresh Peer & Volunteer Mentorship Portal**:
   - Fresh look ready for statewide tutor onboarding.
   - Classification between Students (Tutees), High School Peer Tutors, and Adult Volunteer Mentors.
   - 5-Pillar Matching Algorithm (Academic, Communication/Language, Logistics, Goals, Community).

4. **The Academic Ecosystem (Academic Tracks)**:
   - STEM & Mathematics, English Language & ESL/ELL, Virginia SOL Mastery, Social Studies & Civics, and College & Career / CTE.

5. **Virginia Opportunities & Student Resources**:
   - Science Fair & VJAS, FBLA Virginia, TSA Virginia, DECA Virginia, Invention Convention VA, National STEM Festival, and Governor's Schools.

6. **Leadership & About Us**:
   - Spotlights for Founder Nik Pirouz (with headshot & direct phone) and Board Chair Sean Murphy (with board email `admin@b2sva.org`).
